I'm a final-year Computer Science (AI & ML) student at Annamalai University with a habit of turning coursework into hardware. My final-year project, BuildScan Rover, fuses robotics, IoT, computer vision and deep learning into an autonomous inspection robot — the work was accepted and published at ICICEA'26 in April 2026.

Outside the lab, I lead as General Secretary of CSEA at the Department of Computer Science & Engineering, coordinating 80+ office bearers across technical symposiums, AI summits and department-wide events. I've also volunteered to build a free academic utility platform used by students across 13 departments, and interned on generative AI systems at Live Stream Technologies, Coimbatore.

I care about shipping things that work end-to-end — from a YOLO model running on an NVIDIA RTX 3050, to the ESP32-CAM streaming its feed, to the PDF report that lands in someone's inbox. Currently exploring LLM fine-tuning, agentic AI and MLOps.
